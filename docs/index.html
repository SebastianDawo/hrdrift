Heart rate drift test

Drop a run and find out whether the effort was above or below your aerobic threshold — the field test used by Uphill Athlete and Evoke Endurance.

Drop a .tcx or .gpx file here or click to choose · Strava exports work as-is

Analysed entirely in your browser. The file is never uploaded anywhere.

A drift test is 15 min of warm-up, then 60 min of steady effort on flat ground or a treadmill, holding either heart rate or pace constant. Drift under 5%/hour means the effort was at or below your aerobic threshold.
